<a id="ASEN_2802_RALPHIE"></a>

## ASEN_2802_RALPHIE - Aerospace Sciences Lab redesign and AI comparative analysis

**Short description:** Bobby led durable large-enrollment ASEN 2802 laboratory development and later added the RA.I.phie framework, requiring students to validate standardized AI-generated engineering solutions against theory, boundary conditions, experiments, and their own analyses.

**Long description:** Beginning with the 2022 transition from ASEN 2001/2002 material, Bobby developed and repeatedly delivered balloon, aerodynamics, structures, and aeroelasticity work at substantial scale, supported by LA teams and custom hardware. In Fall 2024 and Spring 2025, the RA.I.phie comparative-analysis framework turned AI output into an object of disciplined critique rather than an answer authority. The paper documents 315 students and 97 voluntary reflections showing numerical comparisons, graph and code inspection, hand checks, boundary-condition reasoning, error reconciliation, and increased confidence evaluating external tools. This is one of the clearest long-term arcs from curriculum creation to reflective redesign, student-scale impact, and national dissemination.

**Primary sources:** Supporting materials are retained in the private promotion repository.

<a id="ASEN_1030_FEEDBACK"></a>

## ASEN_1030_FEEDBACK - Human-reviewed personalized feedback at scale

**Short description:** Bobby's Canvas-integrated AI workflow helped a human grading team return individualized feedback to approximately 100 ASEN 1030 students within 24 hours while processing 969 submissions at very low cost.

**Long description:** The pipeline pulled open-ended concept and coding responses from Canvas, generated course-aligned feedback, displayed drafts for human review and correction, and returned personalized reports through Canvas. Median human review time was 0.44-0.91 minutes per response, median throughput was about 84 words per grader-minute, and roughly 75%-80% of drafts for four graders were released unchanged. The award nomination reports costs under $0.50 per assignment and approximately $7.50 for the term. The strongest framing is not automation: the workflow augmented the instructional team, preserved human responsibility, and made a previously impractical feedback practice feasible.

**Primary sources:** Supporting materials are retained in the private promotion repository.
