## Pedagogical ethos

1. **Durable curriculum leadership:** ASEN 2802 established large-scale experiential curriculum; ASEN 1030 redesigned introductory computing around reasoning and authentic assessment; ASEN 3501 carries the same principles into model validation and experimental methods.
2. **Responsible AI augmentation with measured results:** Bobby did not merely advocate for AI. He built, deployed, measured, criticized, improved, and governed AI-enabled teaching systems while keeping humans responsible and students increasingly able to choose individualized AI use.
3. **Influence beyond the assigned classroom:** The pedagogy transferred into Mathematics and Law, informed required CU System ChatGPT EDU training, earned two CU System recognitions, and entered regional/national teaching discussions through ASEE, Kiewit, RMAIIG, MathWorks, and other venues.
4. **People and capacity building:** LA teams, student developers, SPUR/UROP researchers, high-school students, faculty colleagues, and institutional collaborators developed alongside the courses and tools.
