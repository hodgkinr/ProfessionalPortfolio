<section class="section-band section-band--white" aria-labelledby="foundation-heading">
  <div class="content-shell split-intro">
    <div>
      <p class="eyebrow">Learning-science foundation</p>
      <h2 id="foundation-heading">Scaling what works in individualized instruction</h2>
    </div>
    <div class="prose">
      <p>Educational research has long shown that individualized instruction dramatically improves learning outcomes. Bloom’s 2-Sigma study demonstrated that one-on-one tutoring leads to performance gains far beyond traditional classroom instruction, yet such personalization has historically been impossible to provide at scale.</p>
      <p>BOBPE asks how modern AI systems can help scale effective instructional practices while preserving instructor judgment, pedagogical intent, and ethical guardrails.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

  <div class="content-shell principles-layout">
    <header class="section-heading section-heading--sticky">
      <p class="eyebrow">Core principles</p>
      <h2 id="principles-heading">Technology in service of learning</h2>
      <p>Across every project, AI is treated as a support for understanding and a partner to instructors—not a shortcut or replacement.</p>
    </header>

    <div class="principle-list">
      <article>
        <span>01</span>
        <div><h3>Human-in-the-loop by design</h3><p>AI supports instruction and assessment, but humans remain responsible for decisions and outcomes.</p></div>
      </article>
      <article>
        <span>02</span>
        <div><h3>Understanding over production</h3><p>Emphasis is placed on explanation, reasoning, and reflection rather than automated answers.</p></div>
      </article>
      <article>
        <span>03</span>
        <div><h3>Scalable personalization</h3><p>Tools are built to increase instructional capacity without increasing instructor workload.</p></div>
      </article>
      <article>
        <span>04</span>
        <div><h3>Authentic integration</h3><p>AI is embedded directly into courses, assignments, learning management systems, and feedback workflows.</p></div>
      </article>
      <article>
        <span>05</span>
        <div><h3>Transparency and trust</h3><p>Privacy, FERPA considerations, and responsible use are treated as first-class design constraints.</p></div>
      </article>
    </div>
  </div>
