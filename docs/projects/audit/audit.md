# AI Robustness Audit

## Project Summary

AI Robustness Audit is an AI-informed course review system that analyzes educational content and assessments with special attention to AI robustness. It helps identify where course design is strong, where it may be vulnerable to superficial AI use, and how assignments can be improved for authenticity, rigor, and resilience.

In the broader pipeline, AI Robustness Audit functions as the reflective layer that supports redesign and continuous improvement.

---

## Role in the Broader Pipeline

AI Robustness Audit serves as the evaluation and improvement layer. After a course has been represented and rendered, it helps instructors analyze quality and resilience, especially in the context of generative AI and changing assessment conditions.

This closes the loop in the larger architecture by asking not only whether content can be structured and delivered, but whether the resulting course experience is meaningful, well-designed, and robust.

---

## What It Does

AI Robustness Audit ingests course snapshots and assignment content, prioritizes important materials for review, and generates analyses related to educational design and AI robustness.

Its review process is especially focused on:
- Identifying where assessments may be vulnerable to shallow AI completion
- Highlighting where tasks better require authentic human understanding or performance
- Supporting redesign decisions around rigor, authenticity, and course quality

---

## Why It Matters

The broader pipeline does not end with content generation or learner delivery. Instructors also need ways to evaluate whether their course structures and assignments still support meaningful learning in an AI-rich environment.

AI Robustness Audit helps surface weak points and improvement opportunities so courses can be revised toward deeper learning, stronger assessment design, and more resilient educational experiences.
