# BOBPE Projects

This page provides an overview of the projects that make up the **BOBPE (BOt-Based Personalized Education & Productivity Enhancement)** ecosystem. Each project explores how bot-based AI systems can support personalized learning, feedback, and instructional productivity while remaining grounded in learning science and human-centered pedagogy.

Projects are organized by primary audience, though many span multiple contexts.

---

## Educational AI Pipeline

These projects are part of a broader educational AI pipeline that moves from content structuring, to semantic discovery, to learner-facing delivery, and finally to auditing and improvement.

<div class="callout-grid">
  <div class="project-card">
    <h3><a href="discovery/discovery.html">Discovery</a></h3>
    <p>The semantic search and exploration layer of the platform. Discovery helps users find relevant courses, concepts, and learning materials through embeddings, concept graphs, and source-aware retrieval.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="project-card">
    <h3><a href="forward-lms/forward-lms.html">Forward LMS</a></h3>
    <p>A lightweight course-generation and delivery system that turns structured educational content into modules, lesson pages, quizzes, media-rich content, and learner-facing instructional experiences.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="project-card">
    <h3><a href="audit/audit.html">AI Robustness Audit</a></h3>
    <p>An AI-informed course review system that helps instructors evaluate educational design and assessment robustness, with particular attention to where assignments may or may not remain meaningful in the age of generative AI.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div id="for-students"></div>
## For Students

<div class="callout-grid">
  <div class="project-card">
    <h3><a href="concept-inventory-bots/concept-inventory-bots.html">Concept Inventory &amp; Socratic Tutor Bots</a></h3>
    <p>Interactive AI tutors designed to transform concept inventories from static assessments into formative learning experiences. These bots engage students in Socratic dialogue to surface misconceptions, prompt reflection, and support conceptual change.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="project-card">
    <h3><a href="code-narration-platform/code-narration-platform.html">Code Narration &amp; Interview Review Platform</a></h3>
    <p>A platform that helps students practice explaining and reasoning about code, mirroring authentic technical interview and code review scenarios. AI-assisted feedback emphasizes understanding, clarity, and metacognition rather than code production alone.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="project-card">
    <h3><a href="comp-eng-age-of-ai/comp-eng-age-of-ai.html">Computing &amp; Engineering in the Age of AI</a></h3>
    <p>A course-level initiative exploring how AI reshapes computing and engineering education. This work focuses on curriculum design, assessment philosophy, and transparent AI integration that prepares students for AI-augmented professional practice.</p>
  </div>
</div>

## For Instructors

<div class="callout-grid">
  <div class="project-card">
    <h3><a href="canvas-integrated-chatbots/canvas-integrated-chatbots.html">Canvas-Integrated Chatbots</a></h3>
    <p>AI-powered instructional assistants embedded directly within the Canvas learning management system. These chatbots provide consistent, course-aligned support while preserving instructor control, privacy constraints, and pedagogical intent.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="project-card">
    <h3><a href="latex-lab-grading/latex-lab-grading.html">AI-Assisted LaTeX &amp; Lab Report Grading</a></h3>
    <p>An AI-assisted grading workflow that supports the evaluation of complex technical lab reports. The system generates editable draft feedback aligned with instructor-defined criteria, enabling scalable, high-quality feedback with human oversight.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="project-card">
    <h3><a href="personalized-feedback/personalized-feedback.html">AI-Augmented Personalized Feedback</a></h3>
    <p>A feedback-focused system built on the BUFFALO framework that supports individualized, formative feedback across concept inventories, lab reports, and open-ended assignments. Emphasis is placed on understanding, reflection, and instructor review.</p>
  </div>
</div>
