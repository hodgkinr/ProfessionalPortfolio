# Nomination Submission Requirements

Nominations must contain the following information and adhere to the format outlined below.

---

## 1. Cover Sheet (One Page)

The cover sheet must include:

* **Project Title**

* **Nominee Information**

  * Name
  * Position Title
  * Department
  * Campus
  * Phone Number
  * Email Address

  > If more than one nominee, include the above information for each individual.

* **Faculty Title Eligibility**

  * Indicate faculty title.
  * *Note: Only full-time CU tenured or tenure-track faculty or full-time Instructional Track faculty are eligible to receive an award.*

* **Nomination Type**

  * Indicate whether this is a **nomination** or a **self-nomination**.

* **Brief Description of the Work Being Recognized**
A brief description of the work being recognized.


## 2. Nomination Narrative (Three Pages or Less)

The narrative must include:

### A. Description of AI Use

* A clear description of the specific AI use in the course(s).

### B. Evidence of Student Performance

* Demonstration of increased student performance relative to the learning goals and outcomes of the course(s).

### C. Broader Impact on Student Learning

* Demonstration of the impact the specific AI use has had on student learning beyond the nominee’s course(s).

  > (We understand this evidence may be less rigorous than the results within the nominee’s course(s).)

### D. Pedagogical Evolution

* A discussion of how the AI use is evolving — or has the potential to evolve — the nominee’s pedagogical approach.

## 3. Supporting Letters

*One supporting letter from a student discussing the impact of the use of AI in the nominee’s course.

*One supporting letter from a department/school/college leader.

*No more than three *supporting letters are allowed.
